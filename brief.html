    What happens next

    1. Send the file to your team's designer — Discord, email, whatever you use. It's named after you and your number.
    2. The designer loads it in Clearcoat, which turns your brief into three livery options on your actual car template.
    3. You pick one (or ask for tweaks), and it goes on the car for the next race.
